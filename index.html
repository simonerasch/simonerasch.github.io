about

Simone Cymborski (she/her) is a Danish interdisciplinary landscape architect who works in the fields of architecture, landscape, and art. Through the medias of film, drawing, and photography, her practice explores the points of intersection between different views of nature and interpretations of landscapes - both as territorial and immaterial.
